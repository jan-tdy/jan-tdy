Ever wondered why most of time is DIY cheaper?

For example, you can buy a smart door sensor for ~15€ or do it yourself with an ESP32 for ~8€.

But why?

Because if someone wants to sell it to you, they need it on a PCB, test it, refine it many times before selling, create a nice case for it, develop all the software around it, and then certify it.
When doing it yourself, you just buy cheap components, 3D-print a case for it, play with the software, and done; the only thing you pay for are the components.

Ok, doing it yourself still costs some time, but in money math it is free. But unlike the DIY way, the developers working for a company that sells you a device are paid.
