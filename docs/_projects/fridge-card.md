📋 A Home Assistant Lovelace card for [Fridge Core](https://github.com/jan-tdy/fridge-core)'s AI-recognized fridge contents.

Shows the latest fridge photo (with rotation correction) and lists every recognized item — name, quantity, condition, AI confidence, note, brand and expiration date — each editable right on the card. A checkbox marks an item eaten instead of deleting it, moving it into a collapsed Eaten section it can be restored from. Optional detection-frame overlays show where each item was spotted on the photo, hand-drawn or AI-placed.
