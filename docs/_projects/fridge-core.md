❄️ An ESPHome configuration for an ESP32-CAM that watches the inside of a fridge — video streaming, a toggleable flashlight, and a reed-switch door sensor.

An AI vision automation takes a snapshot on every door open and turns it into a live food inventory To-Do list: name, quantity, condition, confidence and a detection box per item. Anything you edit by hand — quantity, condition, note, brand, or a hand-drawn detection frame — stays exactly as set on the next scan instead of being overwritten by a fresh AI guess. Items no longer detected are left alone; you remove them once they're used up.
